BRAINS Research Center
Brandenburg Research Center for Applied Intelligent Systems, GISMA University of Applied Sciences

I am the Research Director of BRAINS, GISMA's research centre for applied AI. BRAINS covers 17 research areas, from core methods in machine learning, reinforcement learning and graph learning to putting AI to work in business and society. It gives researchers and students at GISMA a shared home for this work and a direct line to partners in academia and industry.

Current work in the centre includes fairness on social networks, how LLM agents behave in groups, fair and multilingual evaluation of language models, and AI for the digital economy. We collaborate closely with colleagues at the University of Cambridge and the Max Planck Institute for Security and Privacy.

If you are a student who wants to turn a thesis into a paper, or a researcher or company interested in a joint project, write to me.
